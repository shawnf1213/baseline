Support

Baseline app and website

Email us: support@baselineev.com. Include the email or Discord name on your membership and, if it is about the app, your iPhone model and iOS version. We answer within one business day.

Signing in

I subscribed but the app says no active membership

Sign in with the same email you used at checkout — choose Sign in with email and enter the 6-digit code we send. If you signed in with Discord instead, your Discord account may not be linked to the subscription; use the email option. Then tap Check membership again in the account sheet.

The code never arrives

Codes are only sent to addresses that hold a membership. Check spam, wait a minute, then request a new one. Each code works once, for ten minutes.

Discord sign-in opens the wrong account

Use Use a different Discord account on the sign-in screen; it asks Discord to let you choose again.

Billing and cancelling

Baseline never handles card details. Where you manage your subscription depends on where you bought it:

  • Bought on the website (Stripe): open the account sheet in the app, or your account on the website, and choose Manage subscription. Stripe's portal handles cancelling, changing your card and invoices.
  • Bought through Whop: manage or cancel it in your Whop hub.

Cancelling stops renewals; access runs to the end of the paid period. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel first.

Deleting your account

In the app: account sheet → Delete account. On the website: your account menu → Delete account. It removes your account record and any notification tokens. See the Privacy Policy for what is kept and why.

Notifications

The app can tell you when a board posts and when a day's results post, per sport. Turn each on or off in the account sheet. If you see no switches, allow notifications for Baseline in iOS Settings first. Nothing else is ever pushed.

Projections and results

Every projection comes from the same model that posts to the Baseline Discord, and every released pick is graded in public. A pick that shows Pending is waiting for the match or game to finish; Void means it did not count (a withdrawal, retirement or a line that changed). Projections are for informational purposes only — they are statistics, not advice, and no outcome is guaranteed.
