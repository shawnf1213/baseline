Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

Baseline (“we”, “us”) operates baselineev.com and an associated Discord bot. This policy explains exactly what we hold about you, why we hold it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. We store the minimum needed to know that you have a subscription and to keep it working. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers of any kind, we never see your card details, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

1. What we collect

Account

Depending on how you sign in, one or both of:

  • Email address — if you subscribe on the website or sign in with an email link.
  • Discord user ID and username — if you connect Discord. We use this to check whether you hold the premium role in our server and to grant it after a purchase.

Billing

Payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own hosted checkout. Card numbers never reach our servers and we cannot see them. We store only what is needed to recognise your subscription:

  • Stripe customer and subscription identifiers
  • Plan (weekly or monthly), status, and when the paid period ends
  • Whether the subscription is set to cancel at period end

The IP address a trial was started from

When a subscription with a free trial is created we record the IP address it was started from, and we keep it in readable form. Its only purpose is to answer one question during an abuse review: has this address already taken a free trial under a different email? A hashed value cannot be eyeballed by a human doing that review, which is why this particular field is not hashed. It is personal data, we are relying on our legitimate interest in preventing trial fraud, and it is deleted with your account.

The free preview clock

Visitors who have not subscribed can browse the app for a short, fixed period. That clock has to survive a refresh, a new tab and a private window, so it cannot live in your browser. We store:

  • A salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address — not the address itself. This table is designed to recognise a repeat visitor, never to identify one, and the hash cannot be turned back into an address.
  • When the preview started, when it was last checked, and how many times.

2. What we do not do

  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, PostHog, Plausible, Segment, Hotjar or anything comparable. There is no third-party tracking script on the site at all.
  • No advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, ever.
  • No card data. See Billing above.

The site uses browser storage for one thing only: keeping you signed in, and remembering small interface preferences on your own device. That data stays in your browser.

4. YouTube

Baseline publishes short daily videos to a YouTube channel that we own, using YouTube API Services. This access is limited to uploading videos to our own channel. We do not request, access, store or process any YouTube data belonging to you or to any viewer.

Because we use YouTube API Services, we are required to tell you that by using those features you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can review and revoke any access you have granted to third-party applications at Google's security settings page.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and billing records — for as long as you have an account, and afterwards only where we are required to retain financial records by law.
  • Trial signup IP — deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Preview hashes — retained only as long as the preview rule needs them.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it. If you are in the UK, EU or a similar jurisdiction you also have the right to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority.

Deleting your account: you can do this yourself from within the app. It requires you to be signed in and to type a confirmation, and it removes your account record along with the data described above. Cancelling a subscription and deleting an account are separate actions — cancel first if you do not want to be charged again.

7. Children

Baseline is not intended for anyone under 21, or under the minimum legal age where they live if that age is higher. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Sign-in sessions use signed tokens, and an account can only be deleted by the person holding a valid session for it. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell subscribers directly.
